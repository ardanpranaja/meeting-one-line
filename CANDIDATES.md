# Candidates

Alternate lines considered for "The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line". The chosen line won because it kills the meeting twice over: the first meeting decided nothing, and its only output was a second meeting about the same nothing.

1. "This meeting could have been an email, but it wasn't even a good email." — Funny, but it leans on the tired "could have been an email" cliché instead of landing its own sting.
2. "We could have just typed 'yes' in the group chat and gone home an hour ago." — Relatable, but it only covers meetings that end in yes; the chosen line covers every meeting that ends in nothing.
3. "Nothing was decided today, except the date of the next meeting about the same decision." — Close second: same recursive-meeting joke, but it reports the failure instead of making the room feel the wasted hour.
4. "We spent sixty minutes saying what a two-line email could have said in ten seconds." — True and relatable, but it reads like a complaint, not a punchline.
5. "The only agenda item was finding a time to discuss the agenda." — Sharp, but too narrow: it only mocks agenda-less meetings, while the chosen line mocks the whole genre.

Chosen: "We just spent an hour scheduling a second meeting to decide what this meeting was supposed to decide."
